Direction 7, of 12

Nightshift

Neumorphism at night. Controls sit in the surface instead of on top of it, so the vault feels like a machined panel rather than a web page.

Palette

night, #14161A

panel, #171A1F

text, #E8EAED

Type

Instrument Sans

One family, light shadows carry the hierarchy.

Shape

9 to 12px radii

Paired inner and outer shadows, no visible borders.

Where it wins

Long sessions

People who want the interface to recede.

Surfaces carry a light top edge and a dark drop. Fields look pressed into the panel. The accent appears on selection and focus only.
